Preliminary Statement - Wrong Loan Addressed in Prior CFPB Complaint Response
This complaint concerns Rushmore Loan Management Services LLC, Loan No. 7602809799,
 secured by 125 NE 110th Street, Miami, Florida 33161, which was sold in March 2021.
This complaint does not concern 1425 SW 19th Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, Florida, or Lakeview Loan Servicing Loan No. 0112347828, nor does it concern any other mortgage loan, property, servicer, or servicing relationship.
A prior CFPB complaint was closed after a response was submitted addressing an entirely different mortgage loan and different property. The responding entity stated that the payoff statement at issue reflected a loan not serviced by them and further discussed a servicing transfer effective June 2, 2026. Those statements relate to a completely different mortgage account and a completely different property.
The mortgage loan that is the subject of this complaint was secured by 125 NE 110th Street, Miami, Florida and the collateral property was sold in March 2021. As a result, any assertion that the loan remained subject to an active servicing transfer in June 2026 should have immediately demonstrated that the response concerned a different loan account altogether.

The prior response did not address the actual allegations presented, did not address the supporting documents submitted, did not address the payoff statement associated with Rushmore Loan No. 7602809799, and did not address the servicing conduct described throughout this complaint.

Accordingly, the prior complaint closure should not be construed as a determination of the merits of the underlying allegations. The evidence submitted in support of this complaint concerns Rushmore Loan Management Services LLC's handling of Loan No. 7602809799 and should be reviewed based upon that loan account and that loan account alone.

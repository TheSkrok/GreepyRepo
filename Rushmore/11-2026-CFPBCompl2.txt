I. PRELIMINARY STATEMENT: PRIOR CFPB CASE DID NOT ADDRESS THE SUBJECT LOAN

I am filing this new complaint because CFPB Complaint No. 26101539898126 was closed without any investigation of Rushmore Loan No. 7602809799.

The response submitted in that matter concerned a different property address, a different loan number, and a different mortgage servicer. The responding entity addressed a loan allegedly serviced by Champion Mortgage/Lakeview Loan Servicing and made assertions regarding servicing events occurring in 2026.

The loan that is the subject of this complaint, Rushmore Loan No. 7602809799 secured by 125 NE 110th Street, Miami Shores, Florida, was paid in full through a real-estate closing on March 5, 2021.

Because the prior CFPB matter concerned a different loan and different servicer, none of the servicing issues described in this complaint were reviewed, investigated, or resolved. This submission therefore constitutes a new complaint regarding Loan No. 7602809799 and not a request to reopen the prior matter.

The response submitted in CFPB Complaint No. 26101539898126 does not address Loan No. 7602809799, the property located at 125 NE 110th Street, Miami Shores, Florida, or the March 2021 payoff transaction that forms the basis of this complaint.

II. CHRONOLOGY OF SERVICING EVENTS AND ACCOUNTING DISPUTE
1. May-August 2020: Continued Collection Activity During Active CARES Act Forbearance

Rushmore approved my COVID-19 forbearance request in writing. Despite the existence of this approved forbearance, Rushmore continued collection activity and transmitted delinquency notices between May and August 2020. During this period, I notified Rushmore's Loss Mitigation Department that its actions were creating financial hardship and contributing to my decision to sell the property.

2. January 2021: Repeated Difficulty Obtaining Final Payoff Figures

After securing a buyer for the property, my title company and I repeatedly contacted Rushmore seeking a final payoff statement. On January 22, January 26, and January 29, 2021, Rushmore representatives provided only estimates rather than an official payoff demand, and multiple transfer attempts were disconnected before resolution.

3. February 2021: Conflicting Payoff Information

On February 5, 2021, Rushmore representative Elijah provided a payoff estimate of approximately $525,269.

On February 10, 2021, Rushmore advised that a written request I had previously mailed regarding the payoff could not be located.

On February 16, 2021, Rushmore issued an official payoff statement reflecting a payoff amount of $526,757 and indicating that the quote was valid through March 17, 2021.

Three days later, on February 19, 2021, Rushmore issued a revised payoff statement that increased the payoff amount. The resulting discrepancy delayed a scheduled March 1 closing and created uncertainty regarding the amount necessary to fully satisfy the loan.

4. March 5, 2021: Receipt of Payoff Funds and Foreclosure Referral

The property closing occurred on March 5, 2021.

My title agent wired $526,802.82 to Rushmore according to its payoff instructions and for the purpose of satisfying the loan in full.

Despite receiving the payoff funds, Rushmore referred the account to foreclosure counsel on the same day and assessed additional foreclosure-related fees and charges.

In subsequent correspondence dated May 4, 2021, Rushmore maintained that these foreclosure-related charges were properly assessed notwithstanding the existing payoff transaction and prior payoff statement.

5. March 11-24, 2021: Payoff Funds Held in Suspense While Collection Activity Continued

On March 11, 2021, after Rushmore had already received the payoff wire, I received a statement asserting that approximately $40,349.84 remained due.

On March 16, 2021, Rushmore representative "Mike" confirmed that the payoff funds had been received but had not been applied to the loan and were instead being held in a suspense account.

Based upon information available to me, the foreclosure-related fees assessed on March 5, 2021 may have created a discrepancy between the previously quoted payoff amount and the amount Rushmore subsequently asserted was owed.

Rather than applying the funds received and resolving any discrepancy, Rushmore continued to treat the loan as unresolved while in possession of the payoff funds.

I repeatedly contacted Rushmore and requested that the funds in its possession be applied to the loan and that any excess funds be refunded.

6. March 2021: Credit Reporting and Short Sale Classification

On March 17, 2021, while the payoff funds were allegedly being held in suspense, Rushmore reported the loan as delinquent and in foreclosure.

On March 24, 2021, Rushmore issued a "short sale acceptance" letter.

I dispute the characterization of this transaction as a short sale. My communications with Rushmore consistently sought a payoff amount sufficient to satisfy the loan in full, and my title company wired the payoff funds demanded by Rushmore for that purpose.

I request an explanation for why the transaction was classified as a short sale and why adverse credit reporting continued after Rushmore had received the payoff funds.

7. Subsequent Disputes

Following these events, I submitted multiple written disputes concerning the account history, payoff handling, foreclosure-related charges, and credit reporting.

These disputes included correspondence directed to Rushmore and its legal representatives, including a written notice sent to counsel K. Rheinholdt on December 21, 2021. The issues identified herein remain unresolved.

III. STATUTORY CONCERNS AND REQUESTED RESOLUTION

The conduct described above raises concerns regarding mortgage servicing practices, loan payoff administration, application of borrower funds, foreclosure-related activity, accuracy of furnished credit information, and compliance with applicable servicing requirements.

Before addressing the merits, I request that CFPB require Rushmore to acknowledge that the response submitted in CFPB Complaint No. 26101539898126 concerned a different property, different loan number, and different mortgage servicer, and therefore did not constitute an investigation of Loan No. 7602809799.

Requested Resolution

Perform a manual investigation and accounting audit of Rushmore Loan No. 7602809799 only.

Provide a complete life-of-loan payment history, transaction ledger, suspense account history, payoff calculations, foreclosure referral records, and all accounting records relating to Loan No. 7602809799.

Correct the loan records to reflect receipt of the payoff funds wired on March 5, 2021, and remove any fees, corporate advances, foreclosure fees, or charges assessed after receipt of those funds that should not have been applied.

Provide a written explanation regarding:

Why payoff figures changed during February 2021;
Why the account was referred to foreclosure counsel on March 5, 2021;
Why foreclosure-related charges were assessed after issuance of a payoff statement valid through March 17, 2021;
Why the payoff funds were held in a suspense account rather than applied upon receipt.

Identify the precise dates on which:

The March 5, 2021 wire transfer was received;
The funds were placed into suspense;
The funds were ultimately applied;
Any foreclosure-related fees were assessed;
Any foreclosure referral occurred.

Correct any inaccurate information furnished to Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion concerning foreclosure status, delinquency status, settlement status, charge-off status, or any indication that the debt was settled for less than the full balance.

Provide a written explanation for the issuance of the March 24, 2021 short sale acceptance letter and identify all facts, records, communications, or requests relied upon in classifying the transaction as a short sale.

Provide written confirmation that Loan No. 7602809799 was paid and satisfied, together with a final statement reflecting a zero balance.

Provide copies of all relevant documents, servicing notes, call logs, internal comments, foreclosure referral records, suspense account records, payoff records, correspondence, and credit reporting records relating to Loan No. 7602809799 from January 2020 through December 2021.

This complaint concerns only Rushmore Loan No. 7602809799 and requests investigation of the servicing, payoff processing, accounting treatment, and credit reporting associated with that loan. The issues raised herein were not addressed in CFPB Complaint No. 26101539898126 because that matter concerned a different servicer, different loan, and different property.

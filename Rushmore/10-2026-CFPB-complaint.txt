YOUR COMPLAINT

I am submitting this complaint regarding Rushmore Loan Management Services due to issues that occurred during the payoff of my mortgage in 2021. After submitting a payoff of approximately $526,800 from the sale of my home, Rushmore provided conflicting information about the payoff amount, continued collection activity after the payoff funds were sent, and later reported negative information to credit bureaus.
Details of the Issue:
* Rushmore issued payoff statements dated February 16 and February 19, 2021, stating that the payoff figures were “good to March 17, 2021.” The February 19 payoff statement reflected a higher amount, and that is the figure my title company used.

* My title company wired the payoff funds in early March 2021, prior to the stated good‑through date.

* Despite this, I received a delinquency/collection notice dated March 11, 2021 showing an amount due of $40,349.84, indicating continued collection activity after the payoff funds were sent.

* In a letter dated May 4, 2021, Rushmore stated that the payoff was “short by $1,508.07” and considered the matter resolved.

* In July 2021, I received a Credit Karma alert indicating new negative remarks on my Equifax credit report related to the Rushmore account, making me aware that the issue was bigger than I initially understood.

* In December 2021, I contacted Rushmore requesting correction of the credit reporting, including the “settled for less” remark. I did not receive any substantive response to that request.

I have documentation supporting each of the above points.
